# Registration / media override

Áp dụng `/registrations` và vùng QR/face của `/households`; còn lại theo [MASTER](../MASTER.md).

## Lý do riêng
Một form multipart nhiều miền (hộ → người → xe) và hai luồng camera browser. Không chuyển thành wizard nhiều endpoint hoặc coi QR là xác minh danh tính.

- `/registrations`: section headings đánh số1–3, progress anchor “Hộ/căn hộ / Người đăng ký / Phương tiện”, **một POST cuối**. Không hidden-step làm native required không focus được. Body tối đa960px, field2 cột khi đủ rộng/1 cột nhỏ; upload và quyền full width.
- Desktop có thể có sticky anchor hẹp trong content, không thêm Bento mosaic; mobile anchor wrap. CTA theo quyền render hiện có: `CARD_MANAGE` hoặc `ROLE_MANAGEMENT` dùng “Lưu hồ sơ và tiếp tục tạo gói”; trường hợp khác chỉ “Lưu hồ sơ”, không hứa mở tạo gói. Sau lưu, chỉ người có hai quyền trên mới sang `/cards?vehicleId=…`; trường hợp khác về `/vehicles?vehicleId=…`. Thông báo thành công dùng “Đã lưu…” để đúng với upsert, không giả định luôn tạo mới.
- QR upload/camera và ảnh khuôn mặt là hai vùng độc lập, preview opaque max-height240, ảnh `object-fit:contain`; trạng thái “Đang đọc / Đã điền, hãy kiểm tra / Không đọc được”. QR không badge Verified. File input có label; lỗi10MB sát field.
- Camera chỉ start khi bấm; có stop/cancel, permission denied + upload fallback. Dừng mọi track khi close/reset/pagehide; revoke object URLs khi thay file. Không gửi camera stream browser sang FastAPI; QR POST qua Spring. Không nhầm camera đăng ký browser với camera gate trên máy ANPR.
- Giữ precedence `registrationFaceImage`/`registrationFaceCapture` và multipart `registrationImage`; không đưa base64/CCCD raw/ảnh vào localStorage, query string, analytics hoặc logs. Form reset/chuyển bản ghi phải clear capture cũ. Sau lỗi server không hứa khôi phục file picker; password/file/secret không persist.
- Backend không trả field errors hoặc lưu draft. Khi JavaScript enhancement còn nguyên document, có thể giữ values/files trong DOM để người dùng xử lý lỗi đã biết; không persist qua reload/session loss, không dựng errors không có trong response và không tự thêm backend draft API.

## G4B-REG-FE — pre-build basis (2026-10-31)

This is the authorized Frontend Build direction, recorded before registration source edits. It inherits the LumiPark MASTER and the decisions above; it is not a claim of rendered quality or user acceptance.

- Replace the legacy `app.css` registration presentation with the existing LumiPark dashboard shell, its approved light blue–violet glass materials, Be Vietnam Pro, local Lucide assets and page-scoped registration styling. Keep the content form-focused, max-width 960px, one column on narrow screens and two columns only where fields remain readable; no KPI/Bento treatment.
- Keep the three numbered sections and one ordinary multipart form/POST. A compact in-page section navigation uses real anchors and remains in normal flow so it cannot cover required fields or focused content. Mark required fields in text as well as with native constraints; keep native form behavior when JavaScript is unavailable.
- QR remains an optional data-entry aid: upload/camera → server response preview → explicit “Áp dụng” action. Do not render a verified/identity badge, apply response values before review, log/store QR data, or put personal data in a URL. A permission/CSRF/non-JSON/oversize failure leaves manual entry available.
- Face image upload and capture are exclusive sources: switching source clears the other submitted value; replacing a file revokes its prior preview URL. Camera activation stays behind a user action and exposes stop/cancel, permission-denied/upload fallback and lifecycle cleanup. Both media areas use contained, opaque previews capped at 240px.
- Keep the existing field names, enum values, 10 MB per-image client limit, CSRF protection and the server-selected post-save destination. While the page remains loaded, a known `400`/`409`/`413` response may show the actual server message without discarding the in-memory form; a `503`, network failure or expired-session redirect is not replayed automatically. Do not persist a draft or promise recovery across reload/session loss.
- Pre-build UX research found required-field clarity, submit-state feedback, inline errors and a focusable linked client-validation summary useful for this long form. No search result establishes QR verification semantics or browser camera behavior; the existing project override and official browser API guidance remain authoritative for those decisions.
- **Observed pre-change baseline:** isolated Edge screenshots at desktop 1440px, mobile 390px and narrow 320px showed the legacy header/form, crowded upload actions and four very narrow field columns on both mobile widths. `review.json` recorded one rendered form, one CSRF hidden field, zero page errors and no horizontal page overflow. This is synthetic fixture observation, not acceptance or interaction verification; details are in `RESEARCH.md`.

## G4B-REG-FE — final refinement basis (2026-10-31, before this code pass)

This is a new, separately requested frontend refinement. It does not rewrite the prior chronology: the previous `ui-ux-pro-max` load happened after implementation had begun and remains a recorded workflow deviation. For this pass, the skill router and `ui-ux-pro-max` lead were loaded before researching or editing source; this basis records the decisions for the current pass only.

- **Grouped validation:** retain native constraints, inline field messages connected with `aria-describedby`, and focus the error summary after submit. Replace its repeated full-message list with three compact, keyboard-operable `<details>` groups matching Hộ/căn hộ, Người đăng ký, and Phương tiện. Each header shows a count; its short field-label links move focus to the corresponding control. Keep the first failing group open, other groups collapsed, and do not repeat the inline message text in the summary. Section navigation may mirror group error counts without changing field order.
- **Media confidence states:** each submitted image shows its actual selected filename/source and byte size, a loading preview placeholder, a decoded preview, or an explicit preview-unavailable fallback. A preview decode error is not silently represented as success and does not discard a still-selected file; state copy distinguishes absent, selected-but-not-reviewed (QR), ready-to-submit (face/vehicle), scanning, applied, and request/file errors. QR remains optional and review-before-apply; face upload and capture remain mutually exclusive, and only the actual retained FormData source is described as ready.
- **Grant-aware save:** render the existing card-management CTA only when `currentAuthorities` contains `CARD_MANAGE` or `ROLE_MANAGEMENT`; render neutral “Lưu hồ sơ” and explain the permission-dependent next step for other users. Do not infer grants in JavaScript or change server redirect.
- **Write recovery:** display distinct known validation (`400`), conflict (`409`), and upload-limit (`413`) messages while keeping the current document and selected files. An unknown (`503`, network/unknown response) keeps the existing no-replay lock, removes any reload-to-check action/copy, explicitly states that this page has no status lookup, preserves the entered form in the open document, and warns before navigation that would discard it. No status is claimed without a backend mechanism.
- **Vietnamese dates:** show one editable `dd/MM/yyyy` text field with numeric keypad and simple separator formatting; validate the actual calendar date and map it to the existing named `LocalDate` value as ISO `yyyy-MM-dd` at the form boundary. Keep the existing native `type="date"` control as the no-JavaScript fallback, and convert reviewed QR ISO values into the visible Vietnamese format. The fields stay optional; add no business date limits. MDN documents that native date input appearance varies by browser/OS locale while its value is normalized to `yyyy-mm-dd` ([official reference](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/input/date)); that is why the display and backend value are kept separate.
- **Visual direction:** keep the already accepted shell, section order, glass surfaces and narrow-screen layout. Refine only the validation summary, three-part progress/status cues, media preview states and save feedback using existing tokens/local Lucide and restrained transitions. No new dependency, global component system, workflow or endpoint.

**Research provenance for this pass:** focused local UX searches ran under Python 3.12.10 and are retained at `.artifacts/tool-runs/20261031T110449Z-g4b-reg-fe-final-research/`. “Focusable Error Summary” and “Error Placement” matched the grouped-summary/inline-error decision. The media-state and unknown-outcome searches had no targeted safe match after one narrower retry; those decisions therefore follow this approved override and observed Backend `400/409/413/503` contract, not generic search results. The `html-tailwind` date query also returned no relevant result after retry; the official MDN date-input reference above supplies the version-independent browser behavior. No basis in this section is user acceptance or a change to prior Build evidence.
